Line weight is log-scaled against each link's capacity (PCIe at its maximum trained rate); numbers are smoothed over ~3 s, sparklines show the last 60 s raw. Disk and network bytes come from eBPF tracepoints (block_bio_queue, net_dev_start_xmit, netif_receive_skb), with process attribution from socket kprobes. DRAM traffic comes from the AMD data-fabric PMU and includes CPU and iGPU. The dGPU is polled through NVML only while it is already awake.
